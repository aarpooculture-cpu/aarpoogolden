About Aarpoo Culture

Every Malayali has two homes.

One is where they live. The other is Kerala. Aarpoo Culture is the bridge — translating nostalgia into modern collective celebrations, turning distant heritage memories into active weekend plans.

The Call of Unity

Aarpoo is the high-energy call. Irroo is the thunderous response.

From the traditional snake-boat races of Kerala — a celebration with zero spectators, where every attendee is an active, roaring participant.

Hyper-Engaged Community

03

Premium Live Production

State-of-the-art sound, curated talent, and bespoke visual design at every show.
