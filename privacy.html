Privacy Policy

Transparency and data privacy are core to the Aarpoo Irroo community. Learn how your data is collected, protected, and used across our ticketing portal and brand services.

Effective Date: August 1, 2026 • Version 1.2

1. Overview & Commitment

Aarpoo Irroo Festivals ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy and is committed to safeguarding personal information collected when you visit our website, purchase event tickets, submit partnership inquiries, or join our community networks. This Privacy Policy explains our data collection practices and your privacy rights.

2. Information We Collect

When interacting with our portal, we may collect the following information:

  • Ticketing Information: Full name, email address, WhatsApp/phone number, and total seats selected when reserving event passes.
  • Partner & Contact Inquiries: Brand/Company name, contact person details, business email, phone number, partnership type, and proposal messages.
  • Technical & Usage Data: Anonymized browser type, IP address, device metadata, and navigation interaction data for performance optimization.

3. How Your Information Is Used

We process your data strictly for legitimate operational purposes, including:

  • Processing ticket bookings and issuing unique entry QR passes via email and WhatsApp.
  • Responding to brand partner, sponsor, and venue host proposals.
  • Sending essential event updates, schedule modifications, or venue entry guidelines.
  • Improving website performance, checkout reliability, and anti-fraud verification.

4. Payment Security & Razorpay Processing

All ticket payments are securely processed using Razorpay Standard Checkout using 256-bit SSL encryption. Aarpoo Irroo never stores, views, or records your sensitive credit/debit card numbers, UPI PINs, or NetBanking passwords on our servers. Razorpay processes all transactions adhering to standard PCI-DSS compliance frameworks.

5. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We share data only with verified technology infrastructure partners who facilitate our operations:

  • Razorpay Software Private Limited: Secure payment gateway execution.
  • Netlify & Supabase: Serverless API execution, database storage, and email ticket dispatches.

6. Cookies & Local Storage

Our website utilizes minimal local storage and essential session cookies to remember seat selections during checkout and ensure continuous smooth frame animations. You can disable cookies through your browser settings, though certain ticketing features may be impacted.

7. Data Rights & Contact Us

You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections, or request deletion of your contact records from our promotional lists.
